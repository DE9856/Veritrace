Scope: the Veritrace app (route /: documents, thread, claim check panel) and /how-it-works. Visitor mode: Operate (/how-it-works: Read).
Audience and job: students, researchers and analysts ask a question of their own documents and decide which sentences to trust, check or discard, confirming any verdict by opening its passage. Also shown live on a projector in a viva.
User asks (2026-10-21): replace the "boring and cramped" look with a new world that stays formal; the claim check panel can be opened and closed, closed until a claim is picked, the choice remembered.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every answer is laid out as a Swiss information sheet: a strict column grid, one grotesk, a huge confidence numeral and a threshold scale that shows why each claim got its label. Refuses the chat bubble with highlights, and the previous galley-proof desk.
OWN-WORLD: Black shell rail (#111214) beside a white sheet on a pale grey ground; neutrals from one tonal ramp; one violet (#4f2fd0) only on what can be pressed or is selected; verdict green, amber, red only for verdicts (stated and inferred sentences take a solid underline, flagged ones a broken red underline, never a strike). Archivo throughout, condensed widths for numerals and headings, regular for reading; tabular figures; square corners, 1 to 2px black rules, no shadows at rest.
STORY: The reader sees the confidence numeral and where each numbered claim falls on the 0 to 100 scale against the 45 and 75 cut-offs, reads the answer, picks a claim, and the panel opens with its evidence while the rest of the answer dims.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Black rail left with brand, documents (unnumbered: a number would carry no information, per the craft floor; each row is labelled by file type), model status. Main: a thin top bar on the grey ground stating what the next question searches ("Searching all 4 documents", which changes as documents are ticked) and the Claim check toggle; the question number and check time live in the exchange's margin column and the Claims header, beside what they describe. Each exchange is a white sheet with a 2px black head rule on the ground. On its 12-column grid: "Question N" as a margin note in columns 1 to 4 and the question large in 5 to 12; columns 1 to 4 the condensed confidence numeral, the threshold scale and the counts; columns 5 to 12 the answer at a fixed measure and the numbered claim list (5, not 6, so the answer column and composer share one edge at every width). Below 36rem of sheet content (so beside the docked panel at 1440 the two columns hold) the confidence and scale form one strip above the answer and the counts and steps follow the claims, so the answer stays in the first screen. Composer docked on the ground under the answer columns with a 2px black frame. Claim panel closed.
FORM: Swiss Information Design (International Typographic Style), candidate 7 of 7 on the re-rolled list; seed:3c7ff3425e7f14ea. Raised: one tonal ramp (exposure record); violet only on pressable or selected things (warm consumer app); one fixed 0 to 100 ramp for every score (star atlas); a selected claim dims the rest of the answer (card wall); the answer owns the screen (guide map); fixed reading measure, spare width to the margins (centre-rail setting).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
