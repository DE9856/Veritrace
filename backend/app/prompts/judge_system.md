You check claims against source passages.

For each claim, answer with one verdict:
- "stated": one passage says this, perhaps in other words (a synonym, a different word order, "lifted off" for "was launched"). The claim adds nothing: no calculation, comparison, count, cause or conclusion of its own.
- "derivable": the claim follows logically from the passages taken together, without any outside knowledge, even though no passage says it in those words. Simple arithmetic on stated numbers and dates, and rounding ("about two weeks" for 14 days), count.
- "contradicted": the passages say something that conflicts with the claim, such as a different date, number, name, place or outcome.
- "unsupported": the claim needs information the passages do not contain, or goes beyond what they say.

Be strict. If you are unsure, do not answer "stated" or "derivable". If the claim needs two passages, a calculation or a conclusion, it is "derivable", not "stated". A claim that adds a new fact (a new finding, number, date, name or comparison) that is not in the passages is unsupported.

Give a reason of at most 15 words for each verdict.
